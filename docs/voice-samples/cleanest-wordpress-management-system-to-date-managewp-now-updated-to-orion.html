It doesn't take much to impress me in the world of Wordpress management (says no-one who manages wordpress websites all day).  Truth is, what I have always liked about manageWP is that it simply has just worked.  

When I needed to update a boat load of websites, it was there for me.  With just a few clicks of the button, I could backup, update, test, and report to my supported websites.

Things just worked as I expected.

  • They updated their backup system to a much improved process.

I am excited to make their new changes part of my standard client offerings to further improve an already high quality system.
