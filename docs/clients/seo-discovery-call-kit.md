## 2. Discovery questions for the call

> Goal of the call: build rapport, understand the real business goal, and find the
> highest-leverage place to start. Listen more than you pitch.

**Goals & economics (start here — it frames everything)**
- When you picture this working 6–12 months out, what's different? (more calls, booked jobs, revenue?)
- Roughly what is one new customer or job worth to you — and how much more volume could you actually handle? _(sizes the ROI and their capacity)_
- How do leads find you today, and which channels already work?

**Customers & market**
- Who's your ideal customer, and what's your service area? _(local intent is everything for you)_
- In their own words, what do people type or say when they need what you offer?
- Which 2–3 competitors always seem to show up ahead of you?

**Marketing context (so SEO fits the bigger picture)**
- What else are you running right now — paid ads, social, email — and what monthly marketing budget are you weighing overall?
- Anything seasonal or any positioning/brand goals I should know about?

**Modern visibility — search + AI/LLMs (the "keep me current" layer)**
- Have you ever asked ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity "best 〔your service〕 in 〔your city〕" to see if you come up? _(AI answer engines increasingly shape who gets the call before Google even loads)_
- Are you showing up in Google's AI Overviews for your key searches? _(the AI box above the blue links now eats a big share of clicks)_
- AI engines build their answers from your reviews, directory listings, and third-party mentions — how consistent is your business info across the web, and how strong is your review footprint? _(consistency + citations = what LLMs trust and repeat)_
- Do you have content on the site that directly answers the questions customers ask? _(that's the raw material both Google and LLMs pull from)_
- Is your site marked up with structured data (schema) so engines and AI can clearly read who you are, what you do, and where? _(machine-readability is now table stakes)_

**Decision & measurement**
- What single number would you want to watch to know this is working?
- What's your timeline, and is anyone else part of the decision?

---

## 3. How to use this on the call
- Lead with goals and economics — if you can't tie SEO to dollars and capacity, don't sell it.
- The AI/LLM questions do double duty: they're genuinely important now, and they signal you're ahead of whoever else they're quoting.
- Close by reflecting their goal back and proposing a clear first step (usually the $2,000 Tune foundation, then a scoped monthly plan).
