**On the Spanish Bible versions, I think the answer is already in your own pages.**

I could not find a separate document from Henry anywhere in my files, and I suspect that one lives
only in his hands. But the Spanish translations already on the site solve the same problem, and they
solve it the way Graham did in English.

Graham quoted from several English versions rather than one, and the site's English pages reference
Phillips, the Good News Bible, the King James, the Revised Standard and the New English Bible. The
existing Spanish translations mirror that habit and name the specific Spanish version each time.
Two examples from Siervos o Amigos:

> "...en la versión de Reina-Valera, revisión 1960: 'El hombre que tiene amigos, ha de mostrarse
> amigo...'"
> speakingwellofgod.org/es/recursos-escritos/siervos-o-amigos/siervos-o-amigos-capitulo-3/

> "'Cree en el Señor Jesucristo', son las conocidas palabras en la versión Reina-Valera 1602. 'Ten
> fe en el Señor Jesús', dice la Biblia de Jerusalén 1970."
> speakingwellofgod.org/es/recursos-escritos/siervos-o-amigos/siervos-o-amigos-capitulo-5/

Across the Spanish pages, the versions in use are **Reina-Valera (1602 and 1960), the Nueva Versión
Internacional, Dios Habla Hoy, La Biblia de las Américas, and the Biblia de Jerusalén**.

That is a working precedent your translator can follow chapter by chapter, and it is already
published rather than sitting in someone's files. If it would help, I can pull every scripture
