## Subject: SEO services for your site — how it works + pricing

Hi Chase,

Thanks for the kind note — glad the summary was helpful, and it's great to hear you're thinking about your digital presence more broadly.

Happy to lay out how we approach SEO so you have what you need for your evaluation.

The simplest way to think about it is two parts: getting the **foundation** right (a one-time tune-up), then **ongoing** work to build and hold your rankings over time. For a business like yours, most of the real wins come from **local search** — making sure you show up when someone in your service area searches for what you offer.

**1. SEO foundation — one-time**
This is our "Tune" engagement: a fixed-price **$2,000** technical and on-page tune-up. It includes a diagnostic SEO and page-speed audit, fixing the technical SEO foundation, performance improvements, plugin/asset cleanup, Search Console setup and sitemap submission, before/after measurement, and a handover document. It makes sure the site is fast, clean, and fully indexable before we invest in promoting it — so the ongoing work actually has something solid to build on.

**2. Ongoing SEO — monthly**
This is the work that grows and protects your rankings month over month. For a local business it typically includes Google Business Profile optimization, keeping your local listings consistent, building out location and service pages, a review strategy, content targeting the terms your customers actually search, steady authority/link building, and — importantly — a **monthly report** so you can always see exactly what was done and how your rankings and leads are moving.

Because the right scope depends on your market and your competition, I price the ongoing work after a short audit rather than quoting a one-size number up front — that way you're not paying for things you don't need.

**What to expect:** organic SEO is a 3–6 month build, not an overnight switch. The early months are about laying groundwork; results compound from there. Patience and consistency are genuinely the most important ingredients — and the monthly reporting keeps it all transparent so you can see the return as it builds.

One nice thing on your end: you're already on our Web Care Plan, so your hosting, security, and maintenance are handled. SEO simply layers on top of that.
