02

What actually produces calls.

Most agencies blur this. We'd rather show you our sheet music. In order of impact for a local service business:

  1. Google Business Profile & the map packWhere the phone calls live. Categories, services, photos, posts — kept active and accurate.
  2. Review velocityCount, recency, and owner responses. Also the single biggest input AI engines use when they recommend a local company.
  3. Service & area pagesPages matched to what people in your market actually need, where they need it — the searcher is already holding the problem.
  4. Site conversion mechanicsFast pages, mobile-first, click-to-call where the thumb expects it. Visibility is wasted if the site doesn't convert.
  5. Citations & entity consistencyYour name, address, and details agreeing everywhere — the quiet foundation Google and AI both check.
  6. Content & schema architectureGenuine answers to real customer questions, structured so engines can quote you cleanly.
  7. AI-answer visibilityThe growing share of decisions made before Google even loads — largely a product of doing the six things above well, and worth measuring on its own.
