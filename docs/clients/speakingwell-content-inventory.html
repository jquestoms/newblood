The two thinnest languages. Chinese and Slavic have three pages each and nothing else: the home page, Donate and Privacy. No books, no recordings. If further translation is ever commissioned, those two are where the site is emptiest. Bulgarian sits just above them with a complete fourteen chapter Servants or Friends.

Spanish, in detail

Spanish is the most developed language after English, and the only other one with recordings, so it is worth setting out on its own.

On the Spanish Bible versions. The existing Spanish translations name the version used for each quotation, in the same way Graham quoted from several English versions rather than one. Across the Spanish pages the versions in use are Reina-Valera 1602 and 1960, the Nueva Versión Internacional, Dios Habla Hoy, La Biblia de las Américas, and the Biblia de Jerusalén. Siervos o Amigos chapters three and five are good worked examples for a translator to follow.
