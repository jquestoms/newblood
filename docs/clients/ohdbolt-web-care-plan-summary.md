## Our Update Process

1. **Back up** — a complete backup of the current site and system is taken first.
2. **Update** — upgrades are applied step-by-step to avoid any site downtime.
3. **Test & verify** — every update is checked to confirm the site is healthy.
4. **Done** — your site stays secure and current, with no effort on your end.
